Candy Blast

How to play Candy Blast

Swap two neighbouring candies to line up three or more of the same colour. They pop, new ones drop in, and if the drop makes another match it chains into a combo with a score multiplier. Each level gives you 25 moves to hit the target score, shown on the bar above the board.

Special candies

Match 4 in a row → a striped candy that clears a whole row or column when it pops.
Match an L or T shape → a bomb candy that blows up everything around it.
Match 5 in a line → a rainbow candy: swap it with any candy to clear every candy of that colour on the board.

Tip: special candies trigger each other. Dropping a bomb next to a striped candy clears far more than either one alone, and that is how the big combo scores happen.
