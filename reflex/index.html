Reflex Test

How the reaction test works

Click the panel to begin. The screen stays blue for a random delay between 1.2 and 4 seconds, then turns green. Click the instant it turns green. Clicking early counts as a false start and the round is retried. After five rounds you get your average in milliseconds.

Reference values: under 200 ms is excellent, 200–270 ms is above average, 270–350 ms is the typical human range, and above 350 ms leaves room to improve. Results depend on your device and display, so compare yourself to yourself.
