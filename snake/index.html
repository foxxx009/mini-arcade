Snake

How to play Snake

Steer the snake with the arrow keys or WASD, or swipe on a touchscreen. Every pellet you eat makes you one segment longer and slightly faster. The run ends if you hit a wall or your own body.

Tip: leave yourself an escape route. Early on, hug the border in a wide loop; once you are long, plan turns before you reach the edge instead of reacting at the last moment.
