Stack Tower

How to play Stack Tower

A block slides back and forth above your tower. Click, tap or press space to drop it. Whatever hangs over the edge is sliced off and falls away, so the next block is narrower and the game gets harder. Miss completely and the tower ends.

Tip: drop on the frame the block is exactly over the one below — a perfect drop earns a small width bonus, and consecutive perfect drops stack into a combo.
