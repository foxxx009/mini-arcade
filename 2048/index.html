2048

How to play 2048

Use the arrow keys or WASD (or swipe on a touchscreen) to slide every tile in one direction. Two tiles with the same number merge into their sum. A new 2 or 4 appears after each move. The round ends when the board is full and no merge is possible.

Tip: keep your largest tile in one corner and never slide in the direction that would pull it out. Build a descending chain along the edge instead of scattering big numbers across the board.
