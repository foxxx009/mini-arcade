Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 8, 2026

No accounts, no personal data

Mini Arcade does not ask you to register, log in, or provide an email address. We do not collect names, addresses, payment details or any other information that identifies you.

High scores stay on your device

Your high scores are saved in your browser's local storage so they survive a reload. This data never leaves your device and is not uploaded anywhere. Clearing your browser data erases it.

Advertising and third parties

This site may display advertisements served by third-party advertising networks. Those networks may use cookies or similar technologies to measure and deliver ads, and they operate under their own privacy policies. Mini Arcade does not control those technologies and does not receive your personal data from them.

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. Doing so will not stop the games from working.

Children

Mini Arcade is a general-audience site of simple puzzle and arcade games. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through a third-party advertisement, please contact that advertising network directly.

Changes

This policy may be updated as the site grows. The date at the top shows the latest revision.
